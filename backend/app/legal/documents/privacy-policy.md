# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: January 1, 2024_

This Privacy Policy describes how Portfolio Desk ("we", "us") collects, uses, and
shares personal information when you use the Portfolio Desk web application, APIs,
and related services (the "Service"). It applies to information we handle as a
business, whether about you as an account holder or about individuals whose
information is included in Customer Data.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account information**: name, work email, organization name, and credentials
  you provide when registering.
- **Usage information**: log data, device and browser information, and actions
  taken in the Service, used to operate, secure, and improve the Service.
- **Customer Data**: content you and your users submit to the Service (for
  example, leases, contacts, and maintenance records). We process Customer Data on
  your behalf as described in the Terms of Service.
- **Payment information**: billing details processed by our payment provider. We
  do not store full payment card numbers.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- provide, maintain, and secure the Service;
- authenticate users and prevent fraud or abuse;
- provide support and respond to requests;
- send administrative or transactional communications; and
- comply with legal obligations.

## 3. How We Share Information

We share personal information with:

- **Service providers** (subprocessors) who help us operate the Service, such as
  hosting, email delivery, and payment processing, under appropriate
  confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
- **Your organization**, where you access the Service as part of an organization
  account; and
- **Authorities or third parties** where required by law or to protect rights,
  safety, and the integrity of the Service.

We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service and
for legitimate business or legal purposes. Customer Data is retained according to
your subscription and may be deleted after a reasonable period following account
termination.

## 5. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect
personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure,
and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. International Transfers

We may process and store information in countries other than where you are
located. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
restrict the processing of your personal information. Where we process Customer
Data on behalf of an organization, please direct such requests to that
organization, which acts as the controller of that data.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected in an updated version and effective date shown above.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact your Portfolio Desk account
representative or support contact.
